My Story

Built from the Ground Up

I didn't start in a gym. I started in life — making choices that either moved me forward or held me back. I've been an athlete my whole life, but more importantly, I've been a student of it. I've learned from jackknives and jacklegs, from mentors who told me the hard truth, and from my own body breaking down and building back stronger.

"My approach is simple: clear-headed, hard-working, and a deep understanding of what the human body is truly capable of — and what it can survive."

That understanding didn't come from a textbook. It came from years under the bar, years studying the science, and years coaching real people to results that last — not the 30-minute kind.

The Comeback Story
The Comeback

Three Days
in a Coma.
Back Under
the Bar.

I coded in the hospital. Three days under, tubes everywhere, my family in the waiting room not knowing if I'd make it back. The doctors were uncertain. My body wasn't.

When I finally woke up, I knew two things: my body had come to the edge — and my mind had not crossed it. The moment I could stand, I started training again. Not because I had to. Because I needed to prove this thing doesn't stop unless you decide it does.

That experience changed the way I coach. Because now I know what the real edge looks like. And I can tell you — the only way past it is through.
